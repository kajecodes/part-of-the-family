How we got started

From dog walks to Part of the Family

Part of the Family began in 2023 with a simple idea from 12-year-old Mylo: earning money by walking neighborhood dogs. That idea grew into a pet care business alongside mom, Kirsten, whose lifelong love of animals made it a natural fit. When Matt’s disability brought unexpected changes to our lives, caring for pets offered a new direction filled with purpose and joy. Together, we created a place where your pets are welcomed, cared for, and loved as part of our family.
